This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance and helps identify key business trends and high- and low-performing areas.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 👥 Sales by Segment
Analyzes sales contribution from:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 🌍 Sales by Region
Provides a regional breakdown of total sales and helps identify the regions contributing most to overall revenue.

### 🗺️ Sales by City
Uses geographical visualization to analyze sales distribution across different cities and locations.

### 🏆 Top 5 Sales
Highlights the best-performing products based on sales, helping identify products with the highest revenue contribution.

### 📉 Bottom 5 Sales
Identifies the lowest-performing products based on sales and helps highlight potential areas for improvement.

### 👑 Top 5 Profitable Customers
Analyzes customers based on profitability and highlights the highest-value customers contributing to business profit.

### 📅 Year-wise Analysis
Interactive year filters allow users to analyze performance across:
- 2012
- 2013
- 2014
- 2015

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into an **interactive business intelligence dashboard** that helps stakeholders understand sales performance, customer profitability, regional trends, product performance, and business growth.

## 💡 Business Questions Answered

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- What are the overall sales and quantity?
- Which customer segment generates the most sales?
- Which regions contribute the most to total sales?
- Which cities have strong sales performance?
- What are the top-performing products?
- Which products have the lowest sales?
- Who are the most profitable customers?
- How does business performance change across different years?
